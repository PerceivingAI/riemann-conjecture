## Statement

Finite multiplicative convolution identities do not create additional oscillatory dimensions for the Laguerre chirp; on standard dyadic Type-II boxes the kernel is asymptotically separable. Moreover direct fixed-interior prime estimates need square-root saving to reach root `1`.

Therefore a conventional Vaughan/Heath-Brown decomposition followed by separate generic Type I/II phase estimates does not provide a weaker route to RH. Any further use of such identities must introduce genuinely new arithmetic cancellation beyond phase geometry and generic coefficient norms.
