# Restricted-support Weil positivity is a genuine unconditional foothold

## Statement

Full Weil quadratic-form positivity on the admissible test class is equivalent to RH, but positivity is known unconditionally on a proper sufficiently small support class. Bombieri reproves the restricted-support result of Yoshida; Connes-Consani provide an operator-theoretic conceptual explanation for the archimedean-place positivity in the prime-free support regime.

This differs fundamentally from Li Gram PSD: there is an unconditional positive base regime from which one can ask a support-continuation question.

## Significance for RH research

Identifies support continuation across prime thresholds as the first positivity mechanism in this project that is not merely an immediate equivalent reformulation.

## Limits

Known small-support positivity does not establish positivity after the first prime enters, let alone full Weil positivity.
