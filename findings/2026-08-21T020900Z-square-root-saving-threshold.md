# Direct fixed-interior prime estimates require square-root saving

## Statement

At a fixed-interior scale `X=exp(4nu/A)`, suppose an unweighted prime-discrepancy estimate has size

```text
X^(1-delta+o(1)).
```

The critical half-weight leaves

```text
X^(1/2-delta+o(1)).
```

To reach the RH-equivalent target `X^o(1)=exp(o(n))`, it is necessary that

```text
delta >= 1/2.
```

## Significance for RH research

A merely nontrivial fixed power saving from Type I/II methods is not enough. The route requires essentially square-root cancellation at exponentially large prime scales.

## Limits

This is a scaling necessity for direct magnitude estimates, not a theorem that every conceivable proof must take this form.
