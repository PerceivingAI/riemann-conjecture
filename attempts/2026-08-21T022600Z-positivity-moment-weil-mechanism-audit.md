## Mathematical setup

For the ordinary Li coefficients, write

```text
w_rho=(rho-1)/rho,
lambda_0=0,
lambda_n=sum_rho [1-w_rho^n]
```

in the standard symmetric sense.

Under RH, every nontrivial zero has `|w_rho|=1`, and zero conjugation makes the Li coefficients real.

For the generalized center `s0>1`, write

```text
A=2s0-1,
x=A log m,
B_0(x)=0,
B_n(x)=L_(n-1)^(1)(x), n>=1.
```

The contribution of one prime-power atom `m` to the generalized coefficient `ell_n(s0)` is

```text
-c_m B_n(x),
c_m=A Lambda(m)m^(-s0)>0.
```

## Derivation / argument

### Step 1 — an exact finite Li Gram kernel

For integers `j,k>=1`, define

```text
K_jk=lambda_j+lambda_k-lambda_|j-k|.
```

Under RH, zero symmetry and `|w_rho|=1` give

```text
K_jk
= sum_rho
  (1-w_rho^j)(1-conj(w_rho^k)).
```

Hence for every finite vector `c=(c_1,...,c_N)`,

```text
c* K c
= sum_rho |sum_j c_j(1-w_rho^j)|^2
>=0.
```

Thus every finite matrix

```text
K^(N)=[lambda_j+lambda_k-lambda_|j-k|]_(1<=j,k<=N)
```

is positive semidefinite under RH.

Conversely,

```text
K_nn=2lambda_n.
```

Therefore PSD of all such matrices implies `lambda_n>=0` for all `n`, hence RH by Li.

So

```text
RH
<=> K^(N) is PSD for every N.
```

This is an exact finite Gram formulation, but it is **not a weaker criterion**: the Li inequalities sit on the diagonal.

### Step 2 — conditional negative definiteness and the Schoenberg/Herglotz semigroup

Extend

```text
psi(n)=lambda_|n|, n in Z.
```

Under RH, for any finitely supported coefficients `c_j` satisfying

```text
sum_j c_j=0,
```

the constant part of `lambda_|j-k|` cancels and

```text
sum_(j,k) c_j conj(c_k) psi(j-k)
= -sum_rho |sum_j c_j w_rho^j|^2
<=0.
```

Thus `psi` is conditionally negative definite on `Z`.

By Schoenberg's theorem,

```text
phi_t(n)=exp[-t lambda_|n|]
```

is positive definite for every `t>0`.

By Herglotz/Bochner on `Z`, for every `t>0` there is a probability measure `mu_t` on the unit circle such that

```text
exp[-t lambda_|n|]
= integral z^n dmu_t(z).
```

Moreover

```text
phi_(s+t)=phi_s phi_t
```

pointwise, so uniqueness of Fourier coefficients gives a convolution semigroup

```text
mu_(s+t)=mu_s * mu_t.
```

This is a genuine structural interpretation of the Li sequence as a negative-definite length / characteristic exponent **assuming RH**.

But the converse is immediate: conditional negative definiteness, tested on coefficients `(1,-1)` at positions `0,n`, gives

```text
-2lambda_n<=0,
```

so `lambda_n>=0` for all `n` and therefore RH.

Hence this entire Schoenberg/Herglotz package is another exact equivalent, not an independent proof input.

### Step 3 — the natural generalized prime Gram contribution is not positive

For generalized coefficients, form the analogous kernel

```text
K_jk^(s0)=ell_j+ell_k-ell_|j-k|.
```

The contribution of one prime-power atom `m` is

```text
K_jk^(m)
= -c_m [B_j(x)+B_k(x)-B_|j-k|(x)],
c_m>0.
```

At the first diagonal entry,

```text
B_1(x)=L_0^(1)(x)=1,
B_0(x)=0,
```

so

```text
K_11^(m)=-2c_m<0
```

for every prime power and every `x`.

Therefore the natural prime part cannot be a sum of PSD Gram atoms. Any full positivity mechanism must compensate negative prime correlations against archimedean/pole structure.

This is visible already at the lowest possible matrix entry and does not depend on asymptotics.

### Step 4 — the same compensation structure is built into Weil's explicit formula

For a compactly supported logarithmic test function `f`, let

```text
g=f * tilde(conj(f)).
```

With the standard pole directions removed, the Riemann-Weil quadratic form has the schematic form

```text
Q(f)
= Q_infinity(f)
  -2 sum_(m>=2) Lambda(m)/sqrt(m) Re g(log m).
```

The spectral side is nonnegative for every admissible `f` exactly when RH holds. Bombieri's work studies this quadratic functional directly and records the equivalence between full positive semidefiniteness and RH.

Crucially, Bombieri also reproves unconditional positivity on sufficiently small support. Connes-Consani later give an operator-theoretic conceptual explanation for the archimedean-place positivity in the prime-free support regime.

This is a **genuine weaker foothold**: unlike the Li Gram matrix, restricted-support positivity is known without assuming RH.

### Step 5 — prime powers enter at exact support thresholds

Let

```text
supp f subset [-T,T]
```

in additive logarithmic coordinates. Then

```text
supp g subset [-2T,2T].
```

Therefore the prime-power term indexed by `m` vanishes until

```text
log m < 2T,
```

or equivalently

```text
T > (1/2)log m.
```

The prime-free regime is thus

```text
T < (1/2)log 2.
```

The first nontrivial arithmetic window is

```text
(1/2)log 2 < T < (1/2)log 3,
```

where only `m=2` is active.

### Step 6 — each prime-power term is a compressed translation operator

Let `H_T=L^2([-T,T])`, `P_T` be interval compression, and

```text
(U_a f)(u)=f(u-a).
```

Then

```text
Re g(a)
= (1/2)<f,(U_a+U_a^*)f>.
```

On `H_T`, define the compressed self-adjoint shift

```text
S_(T,a)=P_T(U_a+U_a^*)P_T.
```

The prime-power term is therefore the quadratic form of

```text
-[Lambda(m)/sqrt(m)] S_(T,log m).
```

This converts support continuation into an explicit operator perturbation problem.

### Step 7 — exact norm of one compressed shift

Decompose the interval into fibers modulo `a`. Along each fiber, translation by `a` moves between a finite chain of points inside the interval. The symmetrized compressed shift is therefore a direct integral of path-graph adjacency matrices.

If

```text
L=ceil(2T/a)
```

is the essential maximal number of vertices in such a chain, the path adjacency norm is

```text
||S_(T,a)||
= 2 cos(pi/(L+1)).
```

At exact threshold values the newly touching endpoint has measure zero; the same essential-norm convention applies.

This formula is elementary and independent of RH.

### Step 8 — exact first-prime perturbation size

Throughout

```text
(1/2)log 2 < T < (1/2)log 3,
```

we have

```text
1 < 2T/log 2 < log 3/log 2 < 2,
```

so

```text
L=2
```

and therefore

```text
||S_(T,log 2)||
=2cos(pi/3)
=1.
```

The exact worst-case operator size of the first prime perturbation is thus

```text
Lambda(2)/sqrt(2)
= log 2/sqrt(2)
=0.4901290717... .
```

Let `alpha_infinity(T)` denote the lower Rayleigh bound of the constrained archimedean/pole quadratic form on the same admissible support space. Then the elementary operator inequality gives the sufficient condition

```text
alpha_infinity(T) > log 2/sqrt(2)
```

for positivity throughout the first-prime window at that `T`.

This condition is only sufficient; it ignores the relative geometry between the archimedean operator and the compressed shift and can therefore be sharpened by a relative-operator analysis.

### Step 9 — why this is the useful mechanism pivot

The Li Gram and Schoenberg formulations are exact equivalents with no weaker base case.

The Weil support formulation is different:

1. there is a known unconditional prime-free positive regime;
2. support spaces are nested;
3. only finitely many prime-power translation operators are active at any fixed `T`;
4. new arithmetic terms enter one at a time at the thresholds `(1/2)log m`;
5. the first-prime operator has an exact simple norm and geometry.

Therefore the natural continuation parameter is support `T`, not coefficient index `n`.

The first genuinely new analytic target is to compare the **actual constrained archimedean operator** against the `m=2` compressed shift, rather than bound either side by global scalar norms.

## Circularity check

Rejected as independent proof inputs:

1. PSD of all Li Gram matrices — diagonal entries are exactly `2lambda_n`;
2. conditional negative definiteness of `lambda_|n|` — it immediately implies Li positivity;
3. positivity of `exp(-t lambda_|n|)` for all `t` — via Schoenberg this is the same condition;
4. a full-support Gram decomposition of the Weil functional — full positivity is exactly RH;
5. an assumed lower bound for the first-prime Weil operator strong enough to imply global positivity without an independent proof.

The restricted-support Weil positivity theorem is not circular because it is already established unconditionally for a proper test-function subclass.

## Result

The mechanism audit is complete.

Established:

1. an exact finite Li Gram kernel
   `K_jk=lambda_j+lambda_k-lambda_|j-k|`;
2. `RH <=> K^(N) PSD for every N`;
3. under RH, `lambda_|n|` is conditionally negative definite and generates a Schoenberg/Herglotz convolution semigroup, but this is again RH-equivalent;
4. the natural generalized prime contribution is not PSD even atom-by-atom;
5. Weil positivity is the relevant compensation framework, and restricted-support positivity provides a genuine unconditional base regime;
6. prime powers enter the support problem at exact thresholds `T=(1/2)log m`;
7. each active prime power is an explicit compressed translation operator;
8. the first-prime window contains only `m=2`, whose symmetrized compressed shift has exact norm `1` and perturbation size `log2/sqrt2`.

No proof of RH has been obtained.
